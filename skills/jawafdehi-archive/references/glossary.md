# Glossary

Nepali and legal terms that come up in Jawafdehi cases and in questions about them. Keep
the Devanagari where it is the real term, and gloss it on first use for an English reader.

## Institutions and bodies

| Nepali | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| अख्तियार दुरुपयोग अनुसन्धान आयोग | Akhtiyar Durupayog Anusandhan Aayog | Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority (CIAA) — Nepal's constitutional anti-corruption body, which investigates and files corruption cases. Commonly shortened to अख्तियार (*Akhtiyar*). |
| विशेष अदालत | Visesh Adalat | Special Court — the court that hears CIAA corruption prosecutions at first instance. |
| सर्वोच्च अदालत | Sarvochcha Adalat | Supreme Court — the apex court, and the appellate forum for Special Court verdicts. |
| उच्च अदालत | Uchcha Adalat | High Court — the provincial-level appellate court. |
| जिल्ला अदालत | Jilla Adalat | District Court — the court of first instance for most ordinary litigation. |
| महान्यायाधिवक्ताको कार्यालय | Mahanyayadhibaktako Karyalaya | Office of the Attorney General — the government's chief legal officer and prosecutor. |
| राष्ट्रिय सतर्कता केन्द्र | Rastriya Satarkata Kendra | National Vigilance Centre (NVC) — the executive body for preventive anti-corruption and technical audit of public works. |
| सामाजिक कल्याण परिषद् | Samajik Kalyan Parishad | Social Welfare Council (SWC) — the regulator that registers and oversees social organisations in Nepal. |
| सार्वजनिक खरिद अनुगमन कार्यालय | Sarvajanik Kharid Anugaman Karyalaya | Public Procurement Monitoring Office (PPMO) — maintains the procurement rules and the blacklist of debarred firms. |

## Offences and case terms

| Nepali | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| भ्रष्टाचार | bhrashtachar | Corruption — abuse of entrusted power for private gain. |
| घुस / घूस | ghus | Bribe; bribery. |
| अपचय | apachaya | Embezzlement, misappropriation of entrusted funds. |
| अनियमितता | aniyamitata | Irregularity — procedural or financial impropriety short of a named offence. |
| दुरुपयोग | durupayog | Abuse or misuse, as of authority or public resources. |
| नक्कली | nakkali | Fake, counterfeit, forged — as in fake academic or citizenship certificates. |
| झुठा कागज | jhutha kagaj | False or forged documents. |
| गैरकानुनी लाभ | gairkanuni labh | Illegal gain or unlawful benefit. |
| गैरकानुनी सम्पत्ति | gairkanuni sampatti | Illicit or unexplained assets — property disproportionate to known income. |
| सम्पत्ति शुद्धीकरण | sampatti shuddhikaran | Money laundering. |
| कालोसूची | kalosuchi | Blacklist — the register of firms debarred from public contracting. |
| बिगो | bigo | The quantified amount at issue in a case — the sum embezzled, the bribe alleged, the loss to the state, or the assets unaccounted for. What it represents depends on the offence, so figures from different cases are not comparable and **must not be added together**. |

## Procedure

| Nepali | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| मुद्दा | mudda | Case, lawsuit, prosecution. |
| उजुरी | ujuri | Complaint — the filing that starts a CIAA investigation. |
| आरोपपत्र | aroppatra | Charge sheet — the formal accusation filed in court. |
| पेशी | peshi | Hearing — a scheduled court appearance. |
| फैसला | faisala | Verdict, judgment — the court's decision. |
| आदेश | aadesh | Order — an interim or procedural ruling. |
| पुनरावेदन | punarabedan | Appeal. |
| वादी | badi | Plaintiff, or the prosecuting party. |
| प्रतिवादी | prativadi | Defendant, respondent — the accused party. |
| चलिरहेको | chaliraheko | Ongoing — a case still in proceedings. |
| सफाइ | safai | Acquittal. |

## Dates and administration

| Nepali | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| विक्रम सम्बत् | Bikram Sambat (BS) | Nepal's official calendar, roughly 56–57 years ahead of the Gregorian (AD) calendar. Nepali court and government records are dated in BS, and Nepal's fiscal year runs mid-July to mid-July. Always convert with `convert_date`. |
| गाउँपालिका | gaunpalika | Rural municipality — a local government unit. |
| नगरपालिका | nagarpalika | Municipality. |
| प्रदेश | pradesh | Province — Nepal has seven. |
| जिल्ला | jilla | District — Nepal has 77. |

## Jawafdehi's own systems

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jawafdehi case | A curated, human-reviewed accountability record built from official sources, published on jawafdehi.org. |
| Court case | A raw record from Nepal's court system, mirrored into the archive. Most of the court docket is ordinary litigation, not corruption. |
| Entity | A person, organisation, government body, court or place, with a stable identifier so it can be followed across cases. |
| Material | A source document behind a case — court order, charge sheet, official report, news article, press release, precedent or procurement notice. |
| likhit | Jawafdehi's open converter for turning Nepali documents into clean Markdown. |
| PNDC | Profit Non-Distributing Company (मुनाफा वितरण नगर्ने कम्पनी) — Nepal's Companies Act legal form for a not-for-profit, and the form the Nepal entity takes. |
| NES / NGM | Legacy internal names for the entity register and the court records. They survive only inside tool names and identifiers — **never use them in an answer**. |
